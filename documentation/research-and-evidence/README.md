# Internet evidence captures

These two images are rendered captures of slides 9 and 12 from the public USENIX SREcon24 EMEA slide deck. They show published PSI/CPU-counter examples and are included for source-context only. They are not AIOS recordings, target-host measurements, datasets, or benchmark results. `Internet_Evidence_Capture_Pack.docx` and `.pdf` present the captures with observations and limits.

Source: [Granular CPU Capacity Management at Scale with eBPF](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/srecon24emea_slides-brighton.pdf) — George Brighton and Cameron Howes, Goldman Sachs, 31 October 2024.

See `Internet_Source_Evidence_Register.csv` for page labels, claim limits, capture date, and SHA-256 values. The published slides refer to the official [Linux PSI documentation](https://docs.kernel.org/accounting/psi.html). A real project-host capture remains unavailable in this review.
