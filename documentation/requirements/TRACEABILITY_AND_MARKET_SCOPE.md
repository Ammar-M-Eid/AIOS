# Requirements and market traceability

**Review date:** 19 November 2026. This note describes the current project baseline and separates proposals from evidence.

## Requirements baseline

The SRS uses 52 hierarchical leaf identifiers. `Ammar_Requirements_Traceability.csv` is the detailed register for requirement names, parent sections, shall statements, priorities, stakeholder assumptions, evidence, verification methods, and status. `AIOS_Requirement_Behavior_Traceability.csv` links requirements to use cases, stories, and sequence views. No legacy-ID crosswalk is part of the current baseline; use the hierarchical IDs for new work.

The SRS is a proposed design baseline, not implementation evidence. Exact APIs, physical schemas, roles, numeric thresholds, cgroup targets, and model internals remain open where identified. Candidate SQLite schemas and EERDs are in `14_Data_Model` and do not constitute an approved implementation contract.

## Market and alternatives scope

`Market_Need_to_Feature_Traceability.csv` contains 18 hypotheses connecting candidate operating contexts to technical evidence, alternatives, proposed features, requirements, and local validation questions. `Market_Alternatives_Traceability.csv` compares six technical approaches. These are research scoping artifacts; they do not establish validated customer segments, market share, adoption, willingness to pay, or product superiority.

Official technical documentation and prior systems research support mechanism descriptions and design constraints. They do not prove local demand, AIOS accuracy, safety, or performance. No local survey/interview responses or TAM/SAM/SOM estimates are included.

## Validation path

Use the anonymous interview template only after supervisor/course ethics direction. Record consent and candidate context without unnecessary identifiers. Update hypothesis status only when actual responses or controlled measurements are available, and preserve contradictory or negative evidence.
